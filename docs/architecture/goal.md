# Kinetic Architecture: Goal

Part of the [Kinetic architecture](../ARCHITECTURE.md).

## Goal

Kinetic is the performance engine of the Aryston series for Minecraft 26.3 on NeoForge with the Vulkan backend. It replaces Sodium and its companion mods (FerriteCore, ImmediatelyFast, ModernFix, EntityCulling, More Culling and Leaf Culling, Lithium, C2ME, ScalableLux, Krypton, Dynamic FPS, Sodium Extra, Distant Horizons) with built-in modules that can be turned on and off, for an ecosystem of vanilla, Helion and Arkea. Render optimization lives in Kinetic; Helion builds its visuals on Kinetic's draw path.
